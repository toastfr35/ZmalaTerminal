# TV brand artwork

These marks identify TV manufacturers. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement
or an implemented/qualified TV driver. The settings carousel is populated from
the bundled driver registry, not from this artwork directory. Unknown brands use
the generic TV pictogram. PNGs are fitted onto a white 256 × 160 plaque by
`build_tools/render-tv-brands.cjs`; source vector geometry is retained.

Samsung, Sony, LG, Toshiba and Panasonic: Simple Icons, commit
`c8a98c7099c33a8ef8461dd97263235f348e7077`, CC0 (see LICENSE-simple-icons.md).
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/c8a98c7099c33a8ef8461dd97263235f348e7077/icons
The renderer applies the catalog brand colour, using black Sony lettering on white.

The following manufacturer wordmarks were obtained from Wikimedia Commons on
2026-11-07. Their file pages classify the artwork as public-domain text logos;
trademark rights remain with their respective owners. PNG changes are sizing,
transparent-margin trimming and a white background only.
